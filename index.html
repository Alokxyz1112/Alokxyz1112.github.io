In development

Now building

A focus and study app for students, on Android. It's built to go head to head with the big players, and it's built for speed.

Block distractions

Block apps and websites, or just Reels and Shorts while the rest of the app still works.

Strict Mode

Optional. Lock yourself into a distraction-free session.

Focus timers

Clock, stopwatch, timer and Pomodoro, with a gesture-driven full-screen focus mode.

A living focus orb

It grows as you focus and blooms when you hit your daily goal.

Rings, streaks and stats

A daily focus ring, streaks, and stats that show how you're really doing.

Day planner

Plan the day, then actually stick to it.

Study chat and calls

Chat with friends, share notes, files and timers, and hop on a call.

Calm sound pack

Quiet sounds to help you settle in and stay there.

Liquid-glass themes

Around 11 themes, each with its own personality: its own liquid motion, glass material and background.

Built natively with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. I'm building it solo: the design, the code and the debugging are all me.
